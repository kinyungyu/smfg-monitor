  • Impact rises to ¥150bn by year 5 as fixed-rate loans reprice
  • Management view: BOJ is “behind the curve” on normalisation
  • Deposit beta: 40% overall, 20% on time deposits — low pass-through protects NIM
  • Further upside from loan volume growth + spread expansion

💰 Shareholder Returns WATCHING

  • Dividend: ¥180/share forecast (40% payout on ¥1.7tn target)
  • Buyback: ¥180bn announced for 3/27 (down from ¥250bn in 3/26)
  • Additional buybacks likely — monitoring geopolitics (Middle East) before deciding
  • Buyback top-up decision at H1 results
  • No major M&A planned — open to selective bolt-ons only
  • If guidance upgrades at H1, dividend could rise above ¥180
Monitor: H1 buyback announcement, payout ratio, Middle East situation

📈 Loan Growth & Spreads POSITIVE

  • 27% loan growth over 3 years → moderating to ~10% next 3 years
  • Large corporates: +17% (now ~40% of domestic loans). SME: +7%. Individuals: -1%
  • Domestic spreads down 5bp to 54bp on large corporate — management says bottomed
  • Margin compression driven by low-margin bridge loans (Toyota, Sony) — will decline
  • Bridge loans generate spin-off revenue: FX, advisory fees
  • Overseas: restructuring to lift ROE from 6-7% to ≥10%. Disposed railcar business, cutting US/EU costs
Monitor: Quarterly loan growth, NIM/spread trends, overseas ROE improvement

⚠ Credit Costs RISK

  • Forecast ¥340bn (up from original ¥300bn). Normalised ~¥300bn/year
  • Gradual increase expected from Asian consumer credit
  • Middle East: ¥65bn provisions + ¥100bn forward-looking. Exposure: ¥3.5tn
  • Private credit: ¥1.2tn (<1% of group exposure)
  • AI/datacentre exposure: ¥4tn
  • SME lending focused on mid-sized enterprises, not micro (less risky)
Monitor: Quarterly credit cost charge, Middle East developments, Asian consumer NPLs

🏦 Deposits POSITIVE

  • Target: +¥7tn deposit growth to 3/27
  • Q1 growth exceeded expectations
  • Retail 46% / Corporate 54%. Corporate deposits +5% YoY
  • Gaining share from regional banks
  • Issued straight bonds for first time in 12 years — diversifying funding
  • Deposit beta 40% = strong NII protection as rates rise
Monitor: Quarterly deposit flows, deposit beta changes, regional bank market share

📱 Olive (Digital Banking) SCALING

  • Accounts: 7.5m now → target 15m by 3/29
  • 50% existing SMBC customers, 50% entirely new
  • Net business profit: ¥20bn (3/26) → ¥110bn target (3/29)
  • Key driver of retail deposit gathering and market share gains
  • Linked to Visa partnership (dates to 1958, Visa stake >¥1tn)
Monitor: Quarterly account growth, new-to-bank %, profit trajectory vs ¥110bn target

📋 Securities Portfolio DURATION BET

  • JGB duration extended: 1.0yr → 3.9yr — big duration bet
  • Shifted from <1yr maturities to 5-10yr held-to-maturity
  • Rationale: rising yields make long-term debt attractive
  • Unrealised losses: domestic bonds ¥259bn, foreign bonds ¥300bn
  • Equity portfolio gains: ¥2.5tn (dwarfs bond losses)
  • Visa stake >¥1tn — exploring ways to utilise unrealised gains. Business relationship “vital to Olive”
Monitor: JGB portfolio duration, unrealised gains/losses, any Visa stake monetisation signals

🏧 Capital & Allocation ADEQUATE

  • CET1: 10.3% (Basel III final, ex unrealised gains). Target ~10.5%
  • RWA growth: ~5% over next 3 years
  • Shifting RWAs to domestic business → profit mix moving to 65/35 domestic/overseas
  • IT budget: ¥800bn → ¥1tn. ¥100bn AI-related, 60% cybersecurity
  • 40% dividend payout + buybacks + organic growth within CET1 target
Monitor: CET1 trajectory, RWA growth vs target, capital allocation changes

🌎 Jefferies Partnership LONG-TERM

  • Announced 20% stake in Jefferies
  • Ambition: full ownership over time — expected to be lengthy process
  • Federal Reserve regulations constrain pace of stake increase
  • Next 2 years: due diligence, management assessment, integration planning
  • Strategic rationale: global investment banking platform + multi-franchise Asia strategy
Monitor: Fed regulatory changes, Jefferies financial performance, stake increase announcements

✈ SMBCAC / Avolon STRATEGIC

  • Scale critical in aircraft leasing — rationale for Avolon acquisition
  • Partnered with Blackstone, Apollo to reduce capital requirements → very high ROE
  • Would not rule out further acquisitions — aiming to close gap with AerCap (market leader)
  • High-return, fee-generating business within group
Monitor: Further leasing acquisitions, AerCap comparison, ROE contribution

  • Middle East: gating factor for additional buybacks. ¥3.5tn exposure, ¥165bn total provisions
  • US tariffs: ¥100bn assumed negative impact did not materialise
  • PM Takaichi: “pro-growth” assessment, positive for banks. Initial team “naïve” on fiscal/debt but “learning quickly”
  • China: reduced exposure over 5 years. Open to fee-based expansion, cautious on loans
Monitor: Middle East escalation, BOJ/Fed rate divergence, yen direction, Takaichi policy

  • Hold until either: (a) valuation de-rates to <1.3x P/TBV, or (b) shareholder returns accelerate materially
  • Compare: MUFG (8306) and Mizuho (8411) multiples for relative value

⇵ What Moves the Conclusion

HOLD → BUY triggers

  • Share price de-rates to <1.3x P/TBV or <12x P/E → valuation gap closes
  • Buyback top-up at H1 takes total >¥300bn → capital return acceleration
  • BOJ hikes 50bp+ → NII uplift ¥220bn+ not priced in
  • Olive accounts >10m ahead of schedule → digital franchise re-rating
  • Jefferies stake increase permitted by Fed → global IB platform narrative
  • Guidance upgrade >15% at H1 → EPS acceleration not reflected in multiple

HOLD → SELL triggers

  • Middle East escalation → provisions consume earnings, buyback cancelled
  • BOJ reverses course / rate cuts → entire NII thesis collapses
  • Asian consumer credit deterioration → credit costs >¥400bn
  • P/TBV >2.0x without earnings upgrade → multiple overextension
  • Deposit beta rises above 60% → NII benefit from rate hikes eroded
  • Yen appreciation sharply → overseas earnings translation loss
  • Jefferies deal terms unfavourable / integration risk emerges
